Recipe Book

  • Rice 3 cups
  • Chicken 1 lb
  • Onions 2 large
  • Garlic 4 cloves
  • Ginger 1 inch
  1. Boil the rice in a pot with water and salt.
  2. Cook the chicken in a separate pot until fully done.
  3. Chop the onions, garlic, and ginger.
